## Scope accepted locally

### P2.03 Spot lifecycle and core binding

- Offline/fake Spot `LIMIT` and `MARKET` lifecycle facts are validated with
  exact decimal quantities, leaves, event identity, duplicate/conflict,
  out-of-order and terminal-event handling.
- The lifecycle-to-core binding is deliberately narrower: only `LIMIT` events
  with explicit strategy role and explicit quote-asset fee can cross into the
  existing `INTENT`/`FILL`/`ORDER_FINAL` core event boundary.
- Partial fill, final fill, cancel/fill race and duplicate execution behavior
  are covered without a second economic posting.
- `MARKET`, rejected/expired states, conditional orders and order lists are not
  forced into the existing limit-based economic model.

Source evidence: `evidence/P2.03/SONUC.md` and
`evidence/P2.03/CORE_BINDING_SONUC.md`.

### P2.04 reconciliation foundation

- Restarted non-terminal attempts are quarantined before economic action.
- Reconnect does not imply `SYNCED`.
- Duplicate/conflicting/out-of-order events, stale state, GAP and reset
  conditions fail closed and require authoritative reconciliation.
- Fake REST/stream disagreement remains a reconciliation condition.

Source evidence: `evidence/P2.02/SONUC.md` and
`evidence/P2.04.c/SONUC.md`.
